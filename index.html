    Beta build. If something doesn't work on your device, copy the report and send it over — it says what your TV reported, and includes your TV's local address but never any credentials.

    Device self-test. Everything above, plus live checks against your TV: it starts a 19-second test video, tries pause, resume and volume, then hands the TV back. Takes about 5 minutes — please don't use the page while it runs.

    To test waking: put your streaming device to sleep first, then run this from your phone. If you use a separate box (NVIDIA Shield, Chromecast, Apple TV…), send the BOX to sleep — switching off the TV picture isn't enough, the box stays awake and the wake checks will skip themselves. Nothing here ever puts your device to sleep for you — a device that ignores wake commands is exactly what we're looking for, and we'd have no way to bring it back.
